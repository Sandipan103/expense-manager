Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

👋 Introduction

Welcome to Expense Manager — Budget Tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application.

We built this app with one core principle: your financial data belongs to you and only you. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

By using Expense Manager, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

🔒 Information We Collect

We collect absolutely no personal information.

Expense Manager does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your financial data, transaction history, or bank information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Any other personal or sensitive information
✅ This app works 100% offline. No data ever leaves your device.

📱 Local Data Storage

All data you enter into the app — including transactions, budgets, and categories — is stored locally on your device only, using an on-device SQLite database (via Android Room).

This data is:

  • Stored exclusively on your phone's internal storage
  • Never uploaded to any server or cloud service
  • Never shared with us or any third party
  • Fully deleted when you uninstall the app

🤝 Third-Party Services

Expense Manager does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK or Meta Pixel
  • Any advertising or monetization SDK
  • Crash reporting services (e.g. Crashlytics, Sentry)
No third party ever receives any information about you or your usage of this app.

🛡️ App Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Used only to send you local reminders for recurring transactions (e.g. monthly salary, subscription bills). No notifications are sent by us remotely.
  • VIBRATE — Used to provide haptic feedback for notifications on your device.

These permissions are used solely for local, on-device functionality and do not involve any data collection or transmission.

📤 CSV Export Feature

The app includes an optional feature to export your transaction data as a CSV file. This file is saved directly to your device's local storage or shared via Android's standard share sheet.

We do not receive, process, or have access to any exported files. The export is entirely under your control.

👶 Children's Privacy

Expense Manager does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data whatsoever, the app is safe for users of all ages.

🔐 Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is managed by your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and enabling screen lock for maximum protection of your financial data.

📋 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
